## Information, not advice

Everything on this site is published for general information. It is not
professional, legal, financial, tax or programmatic advice, and it should not
be relied on as the basis for a decision without your own assessment or your
own advisors.

## Program descriptions

The projects and engagements described here are summaries. They compress work
that ran over months or years into a few paragraphs, and they leave out
operational detail that belongs to our partners.

Where a partner organisation is named, it means we worked with them in the role
stated. It does not mean that partner endorses this website, our other
projects, or any opinion expressed here. Fuller descriptions of an engagement
are available on request, subject to the confidentiality we owe that partner.

## Results and impact claims

Statements about impact describe outcomes we observed in the specific contexts
where the work took place. Development results depend on local conditions,
partner capacity, funding and timing. A result achieved in one program is not a
prediction, a guarantee, or a commitment about what a future engagement will
produce.

Where we say a model has been replicated, we mean the method was applied again,
not that identical results followed.

## Forward-looking statements

Descriptions of intended programs, planned expansion or forthcoming
publications reflect our intentions at the time of writing. Plans change.
